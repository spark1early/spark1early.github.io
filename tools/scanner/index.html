THE ACTION

TVL = pool size. 24h vol = trading volume. turn = volume ÷ TVL — how hard the pool works; higher = more active. %/d fees = daily fee rate.

🔥 HOT = 24h volume is bigger than the pool's entire TVL — unusually high activity, a possible entry signal.

Rule of thumb: high turnover + high fee-APR = real, active yield. A sky-high APR with low volume is usually a token bribe, not a durable payout.

FLAGS

STABLE / LOW IL = safer, low impermanent-loss risk. IL RISK = volatile pair; you can lose to price divergence. INCENTIVE-HEAVY = yield mostly token rewards. SINGLE-SIDED = one asset.

BEEFY · AUTO-COMPOUND = a Beefy vault that auto-compounds an underlying pool. Higher APY comes from compounding (Beefy takes a small fee); vaults show no volume, and they add Beefy's contract layer on top of the pool's own risk.

MY POOLS RANGE & SIGNALS

🔥 HOLDING · active = in range and APR holding. ⚠ CONSIDER EXIT = price left your range, or the 7-day APR is falling — momentum cooling.
